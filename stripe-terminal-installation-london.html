Stripe Terminal FAQs

Why is Stripe Terminal different from SumUp or Square for in-person retail?

Unlike Square or SumUp, Stripe Terminal does not provide a full traditional till software suite on its own. It is a developer-first card reader platform (1.40% + 10p on UK cards) designed to connect to compatible third-party POS apps or custom software built with the Stripe Terminal Software Development Kit (SDK).

Does Stripe provide physical installation engineers in London?

No. Stripe ships readers such as the Stripe Reader S700, S710, and BBPOS WisePOS E directly for self-registration and technical integration. TillSwift handles both the physical London counter installation (Ethernet docks, DNS configuration, VLAN isolation) and the software integration.

Can you write custom POS software or connect Stripe Terminal to our existing booking or e-commerce system?

Yes. As a software engineer, we write production integrations using the Stripe Terminal JavaScript, iOS, Android, and server-driven APIs—linking in-person reader payments directly to your clinic software, Shopify/WooCommerce store, or custom internal web application.

Why do WisePOS E or S700 readers fail DNS or TLS checks on office Wi-Fi?

Stripe smart readers rely on local Domain Name System (DNS) resolution to Stripe's certificate domains and local WebSocket or HTTPS communication. Restrictive ISP routers or guest networks often block local DNS rebinding. We configure compliant routers, Ethernet docks, and reliable fallback.
