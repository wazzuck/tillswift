SumUp installation FAQs

Does SumUp send an engineer to install my till in London?

No. SumUp ships hardware such as the 13-inch Countertop Register, POS Lite, and SumUp Solo for self-installation. TillSwift provides the missing on-site engineering visit across London to mount equipment, pre-load catalogues, connect printers and cash drawers, and test payments.

Which SumUp devices and receipt printers do you configure on-site?

We configure the SumUp 13-inch Countertop Touchscreen Register, SumUp POS Lite, SumUp POS Pro iPad systems, SumUp Solo, Solo Printer cradles, and SumUp Air, alongside approved Star Micronics and Epson thermal receipt printers, RJ11/RJ12 cash drawers, and USB barcode scanners.

Should I stay on SumUp Pay As You Go (1.69%) or move to Payments Plus (0.99%)?

If you process under roughly £2,700 per month by card, SumUp Pay As You Go at 1.69% with zero monthly fee is usually cheaper. Above £2,700 per month, the £19/month Payments Plus tier at 0.99% typically saves money, and our 3-year calculator checks whether SumUp remains cheaper than Revolut Business or direct acquirers.

Can you fix SumUp Solo pairing failures and shop Wi-Fi dropouts?

Yes. Bluetooth and weak venue Wi-Fi are the two most common causes of stalled queues with SumUp setups. We stabilise local Wi-Fi, assign static network addresses to Ethernet receipt printers, and check cellular SIM fallback on Solo terminals.
