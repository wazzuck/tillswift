Custom POS software FAQs

What kind of custom POS software or integrations can you build?

Anything your business operation genuinely needs—from custom Stripe Terminal checkouts and two-way inventory bridges between your till and Shopify or WooCommerce, to bespoke patient billing links, automated Xero or QuickBooks journals, custom label/ticket printing, and multi-site reporting dashboards.

How do you handle complex or unusual business requirements?

We start with an unhurried, thorough requirements-gathering conversation where you explain every quirk of your workflow—split commissions, deposit holds, weighted stock, multi-tier Value Added Tax (VAT), or legacy databases. We then draft a clear written architecture and fixed-price statement of work before writing a single line of code.

Can you rescue or clean up a messy existing till setup without starting from scratch?

Yes. Whether your existing Square, SumUp, Lightspeed, or Shopify catalogue has broken variants, missing tax categories, or disconnected spreadsheets, we write migration and clean-up tooling to standardise everything cleanly with minimal trading downtime.
