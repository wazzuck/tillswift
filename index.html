Direct Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Can you install SumUp, Square, or Stripe hardware that I have already bought?

Yes. Because SumUp, Square, and Stripe ship hardware for self-setup and do not send their own engineers to independent London venues, we bridge that gap on-site. We install, network, and configure hardware you already own—including Square Register, Stand, and Terminal, SumUp 13-inch Countertop Register and Solo, and Stripe Reader S700 or WisePOS E—and test real transactions end to end.

What makes a software support engineer better than a traditional till installer?

Standard till fitters only click through off-the-shelf vendor settings and cannot diagnose network packet loss, write custom integration code, or model long-term merchant fees. With twenty-plus years across software development, hardware, networking, and business requirements gathering, we can build custom API bridges, automate Xero or QuickBooks reconciliation, harden your venue network, and advise you honestly for the long term.

Can you build bespoke POS integrations or handle complex custom workflows?

Yes. If standard app-store plugins fall short, we enjoy sitting down for thorough requirements conversations to design and code exactly what your operation needs—from Stripe Terminal Software Development Kit (SDK) checkouts and multi-channel stock sync to custom loyalty rules, automated end-of-day accounting exports, and specialist clinic or hospitality workflows.

Do you install out of hours so my shop or restaurant does not lose trade?

Yes. We pre-load product catalogues, menus, modifiers, Value Added Tax (VAT) rates, and staff permissions off-site before arriving, and schedule early-morning, pre-opening, or evening cutovers across London so your customers are never disrupted.

Why do contactless card machines and kitchen printers keep disconnecting?

Most venue dropouts occur because payment terminals and thermal printers share congested guest Wi-Fi, suffer from dynamic Internet Protocol (IP) address changes, or sit behind double Network Address Translation (NAT) routers. We wire dedicated Ethernet runs, assign static LAN addresses, separate traffic onto dedicated Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs), and configure 4G or 5G failover.

How much do POS installation and bespoke engineering cost?

Every enquiry begins with a free initial telephone consultation. Standard on-site setup, troubleshooting, and training are billed transparently at £50 per hour, or we issue a written statement of work with a fixed, no-obligation up-front quote for full venue rollouts and bespoke software projects.
