Part 2 · How the 3-Year Payment Cost Model Works

In our free 3-Year Payment Fee Calculator, TillSwift models the variables that genuinely determine merchant processing cost over a 36-month hardware cycle:

  • Monthly transaction count, average ticket size, and trading days per month.
  • Channel split across in-person Chip & PIN / contactless, online gateways, and Card Not Present (CNP) virtual terminals.
  • Card-type mix across UK consumer debit (statutory 0.20% interchange cap), consumer credit (0.30% cap), commercial cards, and American Express.
  • Fixed monthly software/terminal subscriptions, per-authorisation pence charges, and outright hardware costs.

Why the calculator stays impartial and free

The comparison tool is 100% free to run. If a merchant later chooses to sign up with a recommended provider via our referral link, we may receive a standard acquirer referral fee, and we strictly decline any arrangement that would alter provider rankings.
