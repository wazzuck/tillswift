How We Work · Based in Hampstead, London

Two clear workflows: POS engineering and fee maths.

Based in Hampstead and attending venues across Greater London, TillSwift handles end-to-end till design, hardware sourcing, installation, and ongoing care—alongside our free UK payment provider fee engine—always transparently and without sales pressure.

Part 1 · The 5-Stage End-to-End POS Consultancy Lifecycle

Every deployment follows a clear, engineer-led lifecycle—whether you need a single SumUp counter or a multi-terminal venue with bespoke API integrations.

Stage 01 · Phone Call & Survey

Initial Consultation

Free initial telephone call and optional London venue survey covering trade volume, counter ergonomics, printer locations, internet cabling, and bespoke software workflows.

Stage 02 · Architecture & Fee Audit

System Design & Fixed Quote

You receive a complete bill of materials, network plan, 3-year card fee recommendation, and a written Statement of Work with an exact fixed price or £50/hour engineering rate.

Stage 03 · Procurement & Pre-Build

Hardware Sourcing & Staging

We source registers, readers, Star/Epson printers, cash drawers, barcode scanners, mounts, and 4G routers on your behalf—and pre-load menus and Value Added Tax (VAT) off-site.

Stage 04 · On-Site Delivery

Installation & Team Training

Out-of-hours or pre-opening London installation: counter mounting, Ethernet and static IP configuration, kitchen pass routing, live £1 card tests, and hands-on staff cheat sheets.

Stage 05 · Aftercare & Growth

Ongoing Support & Reviews

Long-term support direct from your installing engineer: remote diagnostics, menu and price updates, hardware warranty swaps, extra terminal expansion, and annual merchant fee checks.

Part 2 · How the 3-Year Payment Cost Model Works

In our free 3-Year Payment Fee Calculator, TillSwift models the variables that genuinely determine merchant processing cost over a 36-month hardware cycle:

  • Monthly transaction count, average ticket size, and trading days per month.
  • Channel split across in-person Chip & PIN / contactless, online gateways, and Card Not Present (CNP) virtual terminals.
  • Card-type mix across UK consumer debit (statutory 0.20% interchange cap), consumer credit (0.30% cap), commercial cards, and American Express.
  • Fixed monthly software/terminal subscriptions, per-authorisation pence charges, and outright hardware costs.

Why the calculator stays impartial and free

The comparison tool is 100% free to run. If a merchant later chooses to sign up with a recommended provider via our referral link, we may receive a standard acquirer referral fee, and we strictly decline any arrangement that would alter provider rankings.
