About the Founder

Built by a full-stack software & systems engineer.

I am Neville Bryce — an ex-City software engineer who spent years building high-reliability algorithmic trading systems before bringing that engineering discipline to London business technology, Point of Sale (POS) deployments, and payment economics.

Why TillSwift exists

Small shops, cafés, restaurants, and boutique professional practices across London repeatedly hit the same two problems with tills: opaque, commission-driven fee advice from card sales reps, and legacy installation technicians who cannot diagnose network drops or write software when requirements diverge from a standard template.

TillSwift brings three rare disciplines together under one roof: hands-on counter hardware and resilient Local Area Network (LAN) engineering, genuine production software development, and calm, transparent requirements gathering. Clients return year after year because they can talk directly to an honest specialist who explains trade-offs clearly and builds solutions that hold up under real trading pressure.

Further credentials

Alongside TillSwift, I lead IToldYouSo Computing (London-wide same-day IT support and commercial infrastructure from Hampstead) and Heath & City Technology (sovereign on-premises AI and private IT advisory for King's Counsel and boutique City practices).
